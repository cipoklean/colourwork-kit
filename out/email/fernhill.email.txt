FROM THE DESIGNER

Swatch a few rounds in the round before you start — stranded fabric pulls in at the colour changes. Keep floats loose by spreading the stitches on the right needle as you change colour, and catch the long one on the wrong side.

Wren Holloway · wrenholloway.example/fernhill

Yardage is an estimate for the charted section, +10%; check it against your swatch.
